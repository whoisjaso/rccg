III.  Ministries

A place for every season

No one was made to walk alone. Within the house are smaller rooms — fellowships where faith is sharpened, burdens are shared and friendships are forged for life.

The Fellowships

Iron sharpeneth iron

Proverbs 27:17

  • i.

    Men’s Fellowship

    Men of the Word standing as priests of their homes — built up in prayer, accountability and brotherhood, ready to lead with integrity in family, work and city.

  • ii.

    Women’s Fellowship

    Women of grace and strength, rooted in Scripture — nurturing one another in faith, in purpose and in the joy of serving the Lord together.

  • iii.

    Youth Fellowship

    The next generation, unashamed of the gospel — discovering who they are in Christ, asking the hard questions and finding answers in the Word.

  • iv.

    Seniors Fellowship

    The elders of the house, whose wisdom and prayers are its foundation — honoured, cared for and still bearing fruit in old age.
