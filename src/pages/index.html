How long is the Sunday service?

Sunday Worship runs from 10:30 am to 12 noon. Sunday School, a study of the Word, is from 10:00 to 10:30 am beforehand.

What is RCCG?

The Redeemed Christian Church of God is a Pentecostal church founded in Nigeria, with parishes around the world. Dayspring Chapel is its parish on Hartsook Street in Houston, and everyone is welcome.

Can I watch before I visit?

Yes. Services are on our YouTube channel, and Pastor Funmi shares a daily devotional on Faith Declarations.
