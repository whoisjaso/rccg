A place for every season of life

Within the house are smaller rooms: fellowships where faith is sharpened, burdens are shared and friendships are made for life.

The fellowships

  • Men's Fellowship

    Men of the Word standing as priests of their homes, built up in prayer, accountability and brotherhood, ready to lead with integrity at home, at work and in the city.

  • Women's Fellowship

    Women of grace and strength, rooted in Scripture, nurturing one another in faith, in purpose and in the joy of serving the Lord together.

  • Youth Fellowship

    The next generation, unashamed of the gospel, discovering who they are in Christ, asking honest questions and finding answers in the Word.

  • Seniors Fellowship

    The elders of the house, whose wisdom and prayers are its foundation: honoured, cared for, and still bearing fruit.

Meeting times for each fellowship are shared in church. Call +1 (281) 464-2770 to connect with a fellowship leader.

Church for every age

  • Adult Church

    Deep, systematic teaching of the Word for the mature believer. Doctrine you can stand on.

  • Youth Church

    Worship and teaching in the language of a new generation, without diluting the truth.

  • Children's Church

    “Train up a child in the way he should go.” Bible stories, songs and prayer for the youngest in the house.
