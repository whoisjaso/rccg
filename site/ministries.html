Ministries

A place for every season of life.

The fellowships

Meeting times are shared in church. Call +1 (281) 464-2770 to connect with a fellowship leader.

  • Men's Fellowship

    Men of the Word, built up in prayer, accountability and brotherhood, ready to lead with integrity at home, at work and in the city.

  • Women's Fellowship

    Women of grace and strength, rooted in Scripture, nurturing one another in faith, in purpose and in the joy of serving the Lord.

  • Youth Fellowship

    The next generation, unashamed of the gospel, discovering who they are in Christ and finding answers in the Word.

  • Seniors Fellowship

    The elders of the house, whose wisdom and prayers are its foundation: honoured, cared for, and still bearing fruit.

Church for every age

  • Adult Church

    Deep, systematic teaching of the Word for the mature believer. Doctrine you can stand on.

  • Youth Church

    Worship and teaching in the language of a new generation, without diluting the truth.

  • Children's Church

    “Train up a child in the way he should go.” Bible stories, songs and prayer for the youngest in the house.
