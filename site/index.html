Welcome to the house.

Every nation, every tribe, every tongue.

Look around the sanctuary on a Sunday and you will see the mission statement made visible: one family from many nations under one Lord, worshipping in the house God gave us on Hartsook Street.

We reach

All nationalities of people, tribes and tongues, with the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal salvation.

We teach

The doctrine of Jesus Christ as recorded in the Holy Bible, so every believer lives a victorious and successful Christian life.

First-visit questions

How long is the Sunday service?

Sunday Worship runs from 10:30 am to 12 noon. Sunday School, a study of the Word, is from 10:00 to 10:30 am beforehand.

What is RCCG?

The Redeemed Christian Church of God is a Pentecostal church founded in Nigeria with parishes around the world. Dayspring Chapel is its parish on Hartsook Street in Houston, and everyone is welcome, from any background.

Can I watch before I visit?

Yes. Services and sermons are on our YouTube channel, and Pastor Funmi shares a daily devotional on Faith Declarations.

Who do I contact with a question?

Call +1 (281) 464-2770 or +1 (832) 282-3678, or email info@rccgdayspring.org.
