The Mandate  —  I.

We exist to carry the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ to every nation, tribe and tongue — and to teach His Word until all who believe walk in victory, in this life and the life to come.

i.

To Reach

To reach all nationalities of people, tribes and tongues with the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ, unto eternal salvation.

ii.

To Teach

To teach the doctrine of Jesus Christ as recorded in the Holy Bible — empowering every believer to live a victorious and successful Christian life in this world, before passing into Heaven.

Every Nation Every Tribe Every Tongue One Lord Every Nation Every Tribe Every Tongue One Lord
