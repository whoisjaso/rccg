The pastors

Joined in marriage and in ministry since 1995.

Pastor Joel Uzoma

Saved by Jesus Christ in 1990, Pastor Joel soon became a gifted teacher of the Word. A graduate of Electronics and Computer Engineering, he answered the call into ministry in 1995, studied Biblical Theology and later lectured at the same Bible College.

He has pastored since 1999 and planted churches in Africa and the United States. He is the author of Understanding Shall Preserve You and You Are Coming Out Better, and a songwriter with two worship albums.

Pastor Funmi Uzoma

Pastor Funmi gave her life to Christ in 1985 in Lagos, Nigeria. A graduate of Accounting with a Master of Arts in Ministry, she is an ordained pastor of The Redeemed Christian Church of God and has served beside her husband since 1995.

She is the author of Powerful Confessions for Victorious Christian Living, and every day she shares a devotional, Faith Declarations, with her neighbourhood and beyond.
