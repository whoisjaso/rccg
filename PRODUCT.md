# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build dependencies beyond the zero-dep `build.py` partial assembler. Deploy folder `site/` (Vercel config present). Confirmed by the user.

## Users

Two audiences weighted together:
- **Houston newcomers**: people in South Houston searching for a church, mixed nationalities, often first-time visitors deciding from a phone whether to come on Sunday.
- **West African diaspora**: Nigerian and wider West African families in Houston looking for an RCCG parish that feels like home: the liturgy, the prayer culture, the language of the Redeemed Christian Church of God.

Members also use the site for times, giving and broadcasts, but the site's job is the first two.

## Product Purpose

Turn a website visit into a Sunday visit ("occupancy"). Success: a visitor knows within seconds what the church is, when the next service is, where it is, and feels expected; then plans a visit, calls, or gets directions.

## Positioning

The RCCG parish in Houston that has just opened its own permanent campus (10788 Hartsook Street), led by Pastors Joel and Funmi Uzoma, a couple in ministry together since 1995 who have planted churches in Africa and the USA. Multinational congregation (visible in the dedication photos), rooted in RCCG doctrine and prayer culture (Prayer Warfare, Holy Communion & Power Night, Crossover).

## Product Principles

1. The next service and the address are never more than one glance away.
2. Real people from this congregation, never stock or generated faces presented as members.
3. Honour the denomination: RCCG identity is a welcome sign for diaspora visitors, not a footnote.
4. Opulence through restraint and craft, never through clutter.
5. Every page ends with a clear way to come on Sunday.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Older congregants and first-time visitors on phones: legible type sizes, AA contrast, reduced-motion respected, sound strictly opt-in.
